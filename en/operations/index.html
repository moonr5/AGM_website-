The standard we keep

A vessel is only as trustworthy as the people who take her to sea. AGM crews are professionally trained and experienced in serving industrial, commercial, and tourism principals. Safety procedure is not an appendix; it is the manner of the work.

Shorebase attendance, light logistics, and field support are arranged so that the client is not obliged to assemble a second contractor for the unglamorous but necessary parts of a marine assignment.
