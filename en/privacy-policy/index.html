Enquiry and application notes are kept for as long as they are useful to the work you asked us to do, and then for a reasonable archive period in case of a later dispute or a follow-up. You may ask us to delete a record where the law allows.

Messages are sent to our corporate mailbox. Hosting is on our web server. We do not pass your details to third parties for their own marketing. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Under applicable Indonesian personal-data law you may ask what we hold, ask for a correction, or ask us to stop using or to delete information where that is allowed. Write to the address above. We will answer in a reasonable time.
