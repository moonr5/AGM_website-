Purpose of the vessel

The crewboat is intended for the orderly movement of personnel, for maritime tourism conducted to a professional standard, and for light industrial support where a compact, well-kept hull is the appropriate instrument.

Charter may be arranged by the day, by the voyage, or by the month. The company does not offer the vessel as a recreational toy; it is a working boat, presented with the courtesy due to a principal who must answer for safety and timetable.

Employment

  • Crew transfer to moorings, platforms, and project sites
  • Sea tourism, island passage, and associated guest movement
  • Fishing expeditions under certified command
  • Light logistics, survey support, and field attendance
