New business development

Strategic expansion:
sea cucumber trade

A new business line under development, built on the coastal infrastructure, vessels, and operational network we already run.

Building on its coastal infrastructure, vessel fleet, and maritime operational network, AGM is expanding into the sea cucumber trade — a new business line under development that leverages the company's existing marine capabilities to enter Indonesia's coastal commodity export sector.

This initiative extends AGM's footprint beyond vessel services into harvesting, processing, and international trade — covering the full chain from Indonesian coastal waters to established overseas buyers in Hong Kong.

Scope of the business line

01

Harvesting

Sourcing and harvesting of sea cucumber from Indonesian coastal waters, supported by AGM's marine operational infrastructure and vessel access.

02

Processing

Processing raw sea cucumber into export-grade product, meeting the quality and handling standards required for international trade.

03

Export & import

End-to-end management of export and import logistics, connecting Indonesian sourcing directly with overseas trading partners.

Hong Kong trade partners
