The yard

A principal who commissions a vessel is entitled to know where it is made. AGM builds in fibreglass-reinforced plastic at Marunda, with a yard team accustomed to the particular demands of Indonesian coastal and industrial service.

Geometry, interior arrangement, and engine configuration may be adjusted to the work the boat must do. We favour a durable finish over ornamental haste. Completion is planned with the candour that a serious timetable requires.

What the yard will discuss

  • Custom FRP hulls for transfer, tourism, and support duties
  • Repair, retrofit, and refinishing of existing craft
  • Electrical, navigation, and machinery attendance
  • A build programme stated in writing before work commences
