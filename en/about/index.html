The company

A ship’s company and FRP yard, under one roof

PT. Agara Global Maritim (AGM) is based in Marunda, North Jakarta. We charter crewboats, support marine operations, and build high-quality FRP vessels for industrial, tourism, and fishing principals who must answer for safety and timetable.

We operate charter-ready vessels and expand the fleet through our own shipyard, so quality and build time stay in our hands.

AGM is part of the PT. Stratcon Agara Global (SAG) Group, which gives us added reach through its strategic advisory and government relations network.

Message From Our Director

At Agara Global Maritim, we understand that a vessel is more than an asset—it is an essential part of our client’s operations and success. That is why we work closely with each client to build fiberglass boats tailored to their specific purpose, whether for passenger transport, tourism, diving, fishing, or commercial use.

As Operational Director, my commitment is to ensure that every client receives honest communication, experienced craftsmanship, competitive value, and a vessel they can depend on. We are not only here to build your boat; we want to understand your goals and become a trusted maritime partner as your business grows.

Di Agara Global Maritim, kami memahami bahwa kapal bukan sekadar aset, tetapi merupakan bagian penting dari kegiatan operasional dan keberhasilan bisnis klien kami.
