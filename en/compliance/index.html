How this shows up in the work

Compliance is not a certificate on a wall. It is a build programme stated in writing before laminate is cut, a charter with defined terms and crew responsibilities, and a maintenance record that survives a change of operator. Where a project must be defensible to a board, a lender, or a community, we will discuss methods and documentation rather than rates alone.
