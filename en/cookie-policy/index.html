What this site
stores on your device

A short account of the cookies and similar storage used on agmaritim.com. Last updated 7 September 2026.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website can save on your device. This site also uses the browser’s local storage for the same purpose: to remember a choice you have already made.

Necessary

These keep the site usable. They remember your cookie preference and allow the enquiry and careers forms to send a message. They do not track you across other websites. They cannot be switched off here without breaking those functions.

Analytics

Optional. If you allow them, we may measure how the pages are used so we can improve the site. They stay off until you accept them in the Cookie Manager.

Marketing

Optional. This site does not run advertising tags today. The switch is here so a later campaign cannot start without your consent. Leave it off if you prefer.

How to change your mind

Use the Cookie Manager at any time, or the banner that appears on a first visit. You can also clear cookies in your browser. That will ask us to take your choice again on the next visit.
