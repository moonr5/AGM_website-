A short account of the cookies and similar storage used on this website. Last updated 28 September 2026.

What a cookie is

A cookie is a small file a website can save on your device. This site also uses the browser’s local storage for the same purpose: to remember a choice you have already made. A first visit shows a banner so you can accept optional items, keep necessary items only, or open the Cookie Manager.

What we store

Name Type Purpose Duration
agm-cookie-prefs Necessary Remembers your cookie choice so the banner does not return every page load. Until you clear site data
agm-lang Necessary Remembers English or Bahasa Indonesia. Until you clear site data
Analytics tags Optional Not loaded unless you allow Analytics. None are active on a first visit. Only if you consent

Necessary

These keep the site usable. They remember your language and cookie preference and allow the enquiry forms to send a message. They do not track you across other websites. They cannot be switched off here without breaking those functions.

Analytics

Optional. If you allow them, we may measure how the pages are used so we can improve the site. They stay off until you accept them in the banner or the Cookie Manager.

Marketing

Optional. This site does not run advertising tags today. Leave the switch off if you prefer.

How to change your mind

Use the Cookie Manager at any time. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser. That will ask us to take your choice again on the next visit.
